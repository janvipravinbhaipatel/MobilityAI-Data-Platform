# Business Requirements

## Business Problem

GlobalMove Mobility manages employee relocations across
multiple countries, vendors, and service providers.

Relocation information currently comes from multiple sources,
making it difficult for management to analyze costs,
performance, employee satisfaction, and vendor quality.

The objective of MobilityAI is to create a centralized,
AI-ready data platform that supports reliable analytics and
business decision-making.

## Business Questions

The platform should answer:

1. How many relocations are currently active?

2. What is the average relocation cost?

3. Which countries receive the most relocated employees?

4. Which vendors have the best performance?

5. Which vendors cause the most delays?

6. What percentage of relocations finish on time?

7. What is the average employee satisfaction score?

8. What is the employee Net Promoter Score (NPS)?

9. What are the most common employee complaints?

10. Which services contribute most to relocation costs?

11. Which vendors have high costs but low satisfaction?

12. Are relocation costs increasing over time?

13. Which destinations have the longest relocation times?

14. What factors are associated with poor employee experiences?

15. Which operational areas require management attention?
